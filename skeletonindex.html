The BRS Group at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo, pursues scholarly activity that evaluates human motor behaviors as a proxy for neurological capability. Our research is broadly focused on individuals living with chronic neurodegenerative conditions, and the extent to which engineering tools may be useful in improving quality of life.
